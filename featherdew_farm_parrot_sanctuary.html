🦜 Welcome to Featherdew Farm!

🌱 1. Grow Tropical Crops: Select a seed from your bottom Pouch, choose the Plant Seed tool, and click any soil plot. Use the Water can so crops grow over time or when you sleep!

🍓 2. Care for Your Parrot: Harvest ripe crops and use Feed Parrot to keep your feathered friend full and gain Growth XP. Pet your parrot, clean up messes on the perch with Clean, and toss toys with Toss Toy!

🎓 3. Teach Words & Tricks: Open Words & Tricks to teach custom phrases and acrobatic tricks. Performing tricks earns Visitor Tips (Coins) and raises Intelligence!

🦋 4. Growth Stages: As your parrot levels up, it grows from a fluffy Hatchling (Lv.1) into a Fledgling (Lv.3), a vibrant Adult Parrot (Lv.6), and a Majestic Elder (Lv.10) with a golden aura!
